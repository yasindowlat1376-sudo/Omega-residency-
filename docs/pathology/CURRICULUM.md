# Ω-PATH-STUDY — Developmental curriculum map

**Status: INFERENCE / educational design scaffold, not an official IUMS or Iranian Ministry of Health curriculum.**

The app presents a four-year competency progression so the learner can orient each rotation and track gaps. Exact IUMS rotation order, duration, duty schedule, required case numbers, promotion criteria, examinations and board requirements remain **UNKNOWN** until matched to current signed/dated primary documents.

## PGY1 — Foundations and normal histology
- Normal histology across organ systems; tissue processing, fixation, H&E quality and artifact recognition.
- Microscopy sequence: low-power architecture → intermediate-power relationships → high-power cytology.
- Basic gross-room orientation, specimen identification, safety, triage and supervised descriptions.
- Core biopsy patterns; introductory IHC and special stains; structured case presentation.
- Learning objective: describe what is seen before proposing what it means.

## PGY2 — Core organ-system pathology
- GI/liver/pancreas, GU, breast, gynecologic, thoracic, endocrine, skin, bone/soft tissue and hematolymphoid foundations.
- Pattern-based differential diagnosis, test selection, limitations and clinicopathologic correlation.
- Cytopathology and clinical pathology interfaces; synoptic reporting and staging principles.
- Learning objective: integrate morphology with a focused, hypothesis-driven ancillary workup.

## PGY3 — Advanced integration
- Complex resections, subspecialty depth, molecular diagnostics, advanced hematopathology, neuropathology and pediatric pathology.
- Tumor boards, discrepancy review, quality improvement, research methods and teaching.
- Learning objective: resolve complex differentials, explain uncertainty and communicate implications.

## PGY4 — Transition to independent practice
- Broad diagnostic synthesis, supervised service coordination, consultation, laboratory quality and board readiness.
- Critical-result pathways, communication, audit, competency portfolio and readiness-gap closure.
- Learning objective: demonstrate safe, accountable diagnostic practice within local scope and supervision.

## Learning loop
**NORMAL STRUCTURE → CELL / MECHANISM → MORPHOLOGIC PATTERN → DIFFERENTIAL → ANCILLARY TESTS → REPORT / CLINICAL IMPACT → ACTIVE RECALL.**

Each organ module should eventually include normal histology, common reactive patterns, major neoplasms, grossing principles, microscopy, differential diagnosis, IHC/molecular test selection, reporting elements, common pitfalls and spaced retrieval practice.

## Mapping to IUMS
When official documents are obtained, record:
1. Issuing authority, document title, issue date, version and source URL/file.
2. Exact rotation blocks, duration, required competencies, case log and assessments.
3. Local SOP version and responsible service owner.
4. Differences between national curriculum, IUMS implementation and this developmental scaffold.

Do not infer missing requirements from ACGME or RCPath. Those are comparison frameworks from other jurisdictions.
