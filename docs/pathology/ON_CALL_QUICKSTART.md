# Ω-PATH-STUDY — On-call Quickstart (PGY1)
A memory aid, not a substitute for local policy or the senior/attending.

## Before shift
Identify the responsible senior/attending and escalation route. Review pending cases, urgent specimens, frozen coverage and handoff items. Check PPE, sharps safety, gross-room readiness and equipment. Locate current local SOPs, acceptance rules and emergency contacts.

## Every incoming specimen
1. Follow institutional identity verification; reconcile container, label, requisition, site, laterality and parts.
2. Stop and escalate any mismatch, missing label, ambiguous site/part or chain-of-custody problem. Never relabel from assumption.
3. Before fixation, consider whether fresh tissue is needed for flow, microbiology, cytogenetics, molecular testing or another validated pathway. Ask the senior when uncertain.
4. Assess specimen integrity, fixative and transport; document pre-analytic issues.
5. Follow organ/procedure-specific SOP for orientation, inking, measurements, dissection and sampling.
6. Maintain traceability: container → accession → cassette → block → slide → stain/test.
7. Document direct observations separately from inference.

## Grossing stop gates
Unknown orientation/margins; unexpected lesion/tissue; tissue scarcity/small biopsy; possible infection/special handling; suspected lymphoma/flow-sensitive specimen; complex resection or uncertain sampling map; any task outside current competency or local SOP. Do not improvise sampling. Pause, preserve and consult the senior.

## Frozen section
Confirm request, site, laterality, clinical question and specimen identity through local process. Clarify whether the question is answerable and limitations. Communicate provisional nature; document as local policy requires. This tool never issues a frozen diagnosis.

## Handoff
Pending item → current status → urgency/deadline → material location → action taken → person contacted → next responsible person. Explicitly hand over pending stains, blocks/slides, fresh tissue pathways, critical communications and unresolved discrepancies.

## Tomorrow's minimum viable plan
Ask the senior about local call expectations and escalation path. Review exact local grossing/frozen/triage SOPs before acting. Use checklist only as a memory aid. When tired, uncertain or outside competence: stop, preserve, escalate and document.
