# Ω-PATH-STUDY — Reference map and evidence policy

## Core textbooks
- Robbins & Cotran: general mechanisms, systemic pathogenesis and clinicopathologic correlation.
- Rosai & Ackerman; Sternberg: surgical pathology morphology and differential diagnosis.
- Lester: practical surgical pathology grossing and reporting workflow.
- Odze & Goldblum: gastrointestinal pathology.
- WHO Classification of Tumours / IARC Blue Books: current tumour classification and nomenclature.

Edition, volume, year and chapter must be verified and pinned before a formal claim cites a specific edition. Do not invent page numbers.

## Online references
- Pathology Outlines: rapid topic orientation, differential checklists and reference discovery; secondary educational resource, not a substitute for WHO/CAP/ICCR or primary evidence.
- CAP cancer protocols/checklists: reporting datasets and protocol requirements within their defined scope and version.
- ICCR datasets: international reporting datasets for covered tumour sites.
- ACGME / RCPath curricula: competency-development benchmarks in their jurisdictions, not Iranian regulations.
- ISO 15189: medical laboratory quality and competence framework; implementation depends on applicable national/institutional adoption.

## Source record required
publisher; exact title; edition/version; publication/update date; access date; jurisdiction; scope; URL or immutable local file; source status; derived claim; epistemic state; reviewer; supersession history.

## Authority order
Current Iranian national and IUMS documents for local requirements; current institutional SOP for local operations; WHO/IARC classification for tumour nomenclature; CAP/ICCR for their applicable reporting datasets; peer-reviewed primary studies for evidence questions; textbooks and Pathology Outlines for learning/orientation. Conflicts must be surfaced, not silently harmonized.
