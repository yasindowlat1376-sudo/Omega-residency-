# Ω-PATH-STUDY — Reference map and evidence policy

## Core learning references
- **Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Disease** — general mechanisms, systemic pathogenesis and clinicopathologic correlation.
- **Rosai and Ackerman’s Surgical Pathology** — surgical pathology morphology and differential diagnosis.
- **Sternberg’s Diagnostic Surgical Pathology** — pattern-based diagnostic surgical pathology.
- **Lester** — practical surgical pathology grossing and reporting workflow.
- **Odze & Goldblum** — gastrointestinal, liver, biliary and pancreatic pathology.
- **WHO Classification of Tumours / IARC Blue Books** — tumour taxonomy, nomenclature and diagnostic criteria.
- **Pathology Outlines** — rapid topic orientation and differential review; secondary educational source, not a substitute for primary classifications or guidelines.

Edition, volume, year and chapter must be verified against the edition actually available to the resident before a formal claim cites a specific edition. Do not invent page numbers.

## Reporting and professional resources
- [CAP Current Cancer Protocols](https://www.cap.org/protocols-and-guidelines/cancer-protocols/current-cancer-protocols/) — versioned reporting datasets and required data elements within their scope. CAP content is copyrighted; the app links to official resources and does not reproduce protocol text.
- [ICCR Published Datasets](https://www.iccr-cancer.org/datasets/published-datasets/) — internationally harmonized evidence-based cancer reporting datasets.
- [CAP Current Guidelines](https://www.cap.org/protocols-and-guidelines/cap-guidelines/current-cap-guidelines/) — guideline-specific evidence and updates.
- [RCPath Guidelines and Datasets](https://www.rcpath.org/profession/guidelines.html) — UK professional guidance; comparison resource only.
- [ACGME Pathology Program Requirements](https://www.acgme.org/specialties/pathology/program-requirements-and-faqs-and-applications/) — US competency and training framework; not Iranian regulation.
- [ISO 15189](https://www.iso.org/standard/76677.html) — medical laboratory quality and competence framework; local implementation depends on applicable national and institutional adoption.

## Iranian / IUMS authority
- [Iranian Ministry of Health — Deputy for Education](https://dme.behdasht.gov.ir/) — national education policies and approved curriculum documents.
- [Iran University of Medical Sciences](https://iums.ac.ir/) — institutional notices, educational regulations and local service information.

**Current evidence state:** No publicly accessible, current, signed IUMS Pathology residency block calendar / full local SOP set was verified for this build. The app therefore labels the four-year curriculum as a developmental scaffold and does not present local rotations, case quotas or promotion rules as official.

## Source record required
Publisher; exact title; edition/version; publication/update date; access date; jurisdiction; scope; URL or immutable local file; source status; derived claim; epistemic state; reviewer; supersession history.

## Authority order
Current Iranian national and IUMS documents for local requirements; current institutional SOP for local operations; WHO/IARC classification for tumour nomenclature; CAP/ICCR for their applicable reporting datasets; peer-reviewed primary studies for evidence questions; textbooks and Pathology Outlines for learning/orientation. Conflicts must be surfaced, not silently harmonized.
